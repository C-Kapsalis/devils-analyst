# Breadth run, 2026-10-17

Source log: [../eval-breadth.log](../eval-breadth.log). Method:
[../METHOD.md](../METHOD.md).

- **Cases:** the 36 breadth cases (`bx-*`). Each is a draft report, memo or
  proposal with the exports behind it, four to six planted problems,
  usually two decoys, and a decision to make. All data is synthetic.
- **Runs:** 3 per case per arm, 216 in all (108 with Devil's Analyst, 108
  without). Wall time 3,661 s; API cost $5.98 as printed by the log.
- **Model under test:** Claude Haiku 5.5 in Claude Code.
- **Judge:** the `claude plugin eval` default (Claude Haiku), three votes
  per grader, majority wins.

case's score by a third, and the overall gaps here are one or two runs out
of 108.

## What I read from it

- Without the plugin, Claude Haiku 5.5 already found enough of the planted
  problems in 107 of 108 runs. These cases do not separate the two arms on
  recall.
- The largest gains were on decisions: `bx-commerce-tier-price-rise`
  (without the plugin, two of three runs rolled back a price rise that was
  profitable once season and stock-outs were accounted for) and
  `bx-social-creative-test` (two of three runs without, one with, declared
  a winner from an unfair creative test).
- `bx-pmax-marginal` gained on decoys, not on the decision: both arms
  rejected the budget increase.
- The largest drops were on three SEO cases (`bx-seo-cmo-update`,
  `bx-seo-content-roadmap`, `bx-seo-replatform`) and on
  `bx-brand-social-quarterly`. In `bx-seo-cmo-update`, the with-plugin arm
  presented a decoy as a problem in all three runs, although one of that
  case's decoys (Search Console clicks above GA4 sessions) is on the
  plugin's own Search Console decoy list.
- Decoys are the weakest grader in both arms (about 80%).
