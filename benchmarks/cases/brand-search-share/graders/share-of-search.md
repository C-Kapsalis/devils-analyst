But category searches grew +40% (995,920 -> 1,394,850) and competitor brand searches grew Fjellmark +59%, Arcton +47%, Vallo +22%. Nordvik's share of brand search (own / all brand terms incl. competitors) fell from 22.2% to 19.9%; Nordvik searches per category search fell from 0.121 to 0.111 (-8%). The +30% is the market rising, not Nordvik gaining.
