Decision: make the Academy mandatory for at-risk accounts and hire two trainers, or not.

Ground truth: the only unbiased comparison is the randomised one: invited 70.0% vs not invited 67.0% renewal, +3.0 pts (95% CI -4.4 to +10.4, not significant). Value per invited account is about GBP 90 vs GBP 150 cost; break-even needs +5 pts. (Effect per graduate, ITT / completion rate 32.3%, is about +9 pts; acceptable as context, but the cost is per invited account.) Correct main recommendation: do not make it mandatory or hire for a rollout on this evidence; redesign (e.g. shorter format to raise completion, lower cost) and/or run a larger randomised test against the 5-pt break-even.

Naive wrong recommendation: roll it out because graduates renew 20 pts more, health rose +8 and CSAT rose.
