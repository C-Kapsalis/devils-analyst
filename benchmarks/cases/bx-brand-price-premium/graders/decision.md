The page asks the board to approve a 6% list price rise from January.

Correct call: do NOT approve the 6% list rise as proposed now. The realised premium is only about 7%-9% (not 23%), 47% of revenue already comes on discounted orders, and full-price repeat is falling, so a list rise mostly widens the gap to the discounted price or loses full-price buyers. Acceptable main recommendations: hold list price and first reduce discount frequency/depth (which raises realised price); and/or run a controlled price test (one region, channel or SKU set, with a holdout and a stated break-even volume loss) before any across-the-board rise; or postpone the decision until such a test. Any of these is PASS.

Naive wrong answer: approve the 6% rise because the brand is "#1 with 24% share", has a "+23% premium" and NPS is up.

PASS if report.md's main recommendation is not to approve the across-the-board 6% rise now (one of the acceptable alternatives above).
FAIL if report.md recommends approving the 6% list rise as proposed (even with minor caveats), or gives no recommendation.
