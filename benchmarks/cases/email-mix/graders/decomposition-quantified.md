PASS if report.md quantifies WHY the headline numbers moved with an explicit decomposition, for example: how many points of the click-rate change come from segment mix versus from within-segment changes, OR how much of the revenue change each driver (volume, click rate, conversion after the click, order value) contributed, with amounts that add up (approximately) to the total change.
FAIL if it only lists before/after figures per segment or describes the causes in words without attributing a quantified share of the change to each cause.
